# Awesome-Sports-Performance-Analytics

## Top Sports Performance Analytics Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Athlete Tracking, Video Analysis, Load Management, Tactical Insights & Sports Science*  
**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Sports Performance Analytics**. These tools capture wearable and optical tracking data, analyze video, measure athlete load and readiness, generate tactical and event insights, support injury risk management, and deliver performance intelligence to coaches, analysts, and sports science staff across professional and elite sport.

**Examples** include Catapult Sports, Hudl, Kitman Labs, Kinduct, StatsBomb, Second Spectrum, KINEXON, SkillCorner, Edge10, and Sportlogiq (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for computer-vision tracking, biomechanics, video analytics, open event data, pose estimation, and self-hosted performance tooling — ideal for sports scientists, analysts, developers, and teams building transparent, customizable performance systems.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Catapult Sports](https://www.catapultsports.com/)**  
  Wearable GPS/IMU athlete monitoring platform providing real-time load, speed, distance, and performance metrics widely used in team sports for training and match analysis.

- **[Hudl](https://www.hudl.com/)**  
  Leading video analysis and coaching platform with tagging, playlists, AI-assisted insights, and integrated performance workflows across dozens of sports.

- **[Kitman Labs](https://www.kitmanlabs.com/)**  
  Athlete performance and health platform focused on load management, injury risk, readiness, and integrated sports science data workflows.

- **[Kinduct](https://www.kinduct.com/)**  
  Athlete management and performance platform that centralizes wellness, training, and medical data for high-performance environments.

- **[StatsBomb](https://statsbomb.com/)**  
  Advanced football event data and analytics provider offering rich datasets, models, and tools for tactical and performance analysis.

- **[Second Spectrum](https://www.secondspectrum.com/)**  
  Optical player-tracking and computer-vision platform delivering high-fidelity tracking data and visualizations for leagues and broadcasters.

- **[KINEXON](https://kinexon.com/)**  
  Ultra-wideband (UWB) real-time location and performance tracking system used in indoor and outdoor team sports for precise positioning and load metrics.

- **[SkillCorner](https://www.skillcorner.com/)**  
  Computer-vision tracking company that extracts player and ball tracking data from broadcast video for football and other sports.

- **[Edge10](https://www.edge10.com/)**  
  Performance and athlete data management platform supporting integrated sports science, medical, and coaching workflows.

- **[Sportlogiq](https://sportlogiq.com/)**  
  Computer-vision and analytics company providing detailed player tracking, event data, and insights across hockey, soccer, and other sports.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Roboflow Sports](https://github.com/roboflow/sports)**  
  Open computer-vision toolkit and datasets for sports (player detection, ball tracking, pitch keypoints, jersey OCR) built on modern detection and foundation models.

- **[SportsLabKit](https://github.com/AtomScott/SportsLabKit)**  
  Python package for turning sports video into structured tracking data (players, ball, teams) with multiple multi-object trackers and pitch calibration.

- **[Sports2D](https://github.com/davidpagnon/Sports2D)**  
  Open-source tool that computes 2D human pose and joint/segment angles from video or webcam, with optional OpenSim kinematic optimization.

- **[OpenBiomechanics](https://github.com/drivelineresearch/openbiomechanics)**  
  Driveline Baseball initiative providing anonymized elite athletic motion-capture (C3D) and processed biomechanics data for research and development.

- **[StatsBomb Open Data](https://github.com/statsbomb/open-data)**  
  Free football event data released by StatsBomb, widely used by the analytics community for research, modeling, and visualization.

- **[soccerEye](https://github.com/tigboatnc/soccerEye)**  
  Modular open-source toolkit for soccer footage analytics including field isolation, player detection, tracking, and birds-eye views.

- **[unified-soccer-analytics](https://github.com/FatControlla/unified-soccer-analytics)**  
  Open-source video analytics platform synthesizing tracking, predictive models (xG, outcomes), and reproducible football analysis pipelines.

- **[AthletiQ](https://github.com/milansinghal2004/AthletiQ)**  
  AI biomechanical analysis pipeline (SAM2 + MediaPipe + generative feedback) oriented toward cricket technique and performance diagnostics.

- **[mplsoccer / socceraction](https://github.com/andrewRowlinson/mplsoccer)**  
  Popular open-source libraries for football data visualization, pitch plotting, and advanced event-data modeling.

- **[OpenPlaybook](https://github.com/sagars2004/OpenPlaybook)**  
  Semantic search and exploration platform for play-by-play sports data using natural-language queries and vector embeddings.

- **[SportsReview](https://github.com/andrewjrobinson/SportsReview)**  
  Open-source video delay and frame-by-frame analysis tool useful for technique review in skill and timing-based sports.

- **[Awesome Sports AI](https://github.com/moose-lab/awesome-sports-ai)**  
  Curated collection of open-source sports AI tools, datasets, models, and runnable prototypes across multiple sports.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Pose estimation stacks**: MediaPipe, RTMPose, OpenPose, and related tools for markerless biomechanics.
- **Tracking algorithms**: ByteTrack, DeepSORT, BoT-SORT implementations adapted for sports.
- **Event & tracking data projects**: openfootball, Metrica Sports sample data, StatsBomb/Wyscout community tools.
- **Biomechanics & force-plate analysis** notebooks and OpenSim pipelines.
- **Self-hosted fitness/athlete dashboards** (Endurain-style) and wearable data importers.
- Many community **xG models**, **pitch control**, **space creation**, and **load monitoring** repositories.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Roboflow Sports / SportsLabKit** for video tracking, **Sports2D or MediaPipe** for pose and angles, **StatsBomb Open Data + mplsoccer** for event analytics, and open biomechanics datasets to create a complete self-hosted sports performance analytics stack.
